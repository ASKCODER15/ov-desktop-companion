# Code of Conduct

OV is committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific when discussing ideas, code, and feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Accept constructive criticism and focus on improving the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private data, credentials, logs, or screenshots.
- Keep discussions relevant and welcoming to contributors with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, sexualised harassment, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post private information or security vulnerabilities in public issues.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They should explain moderation decisions where practical.
